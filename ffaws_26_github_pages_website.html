Academic Scope

Bridging Algebra, Graphs & Applications

FFAWS'26 is hosted at the South Campus of Guangdong Technion–Israel Institute of Technology (GTIIT) in Shantou. The winter school brings together leading researchers, postdocs, and graduate students to explore core algebraic structures, spectral graph theory, error-correcting codes, and combinatorics.

Finite Fields & Polynomials

Permutation polynomials, character sums, rational functions, and arithmetic structures over $\mathbb{F}_q$.

Algebraic Coding Theory

Cyclic, BCH, Goppa, and MDS codes, quantum codes, and cryptographic Boolean functions.

Spectral & Algebraic Graphs

Strongly regular graphs, Cayley graphs, Deza graphs, matrix spectra, and association schemes.

Combinatorics & Designs

Combinatorial $t$-designs, difference sets, finite geometries, and tropical algebraic structures.
